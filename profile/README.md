## 🌟 About Shop24mart

**[Shop24mart](https://www.shop24mart.com)** is a premier omnichannel retail supermarket and digital grocery platform. We bring the complete supermarket shopping experience directly to your fingertips — combining high-quality groceries, fresh food, household essentials, and pantry staples with seamless online ordering, fast doorstep delivery, and convenient in-store branch pickup.

Whether you're shopping from your phone at home or visiting one of our physical supermarket branches, Shop24mart ensures you get fresh, authentic products at everyday affordable prices.

## 📱 How It Works

```
  ┌───────────────┐        ┌───────────────┐        ┌───────────────┐        ┌───────────────┐
  │ 1. Browse &   │  ───▶  │ 2. Choose     │  ───▶  │ 3. Pay        │  ───▶  │ 4. Track &    │
  │    Select     │        │    Fulfillment│        │    Securely   │        │    Receive    │
  │ Add items to  │        │ Doorstep or   │        │ Instant safe  │        │ Live tracking │
  │ your cart     │        │ Branch Pickup │        │ checkout      │        │ to your door  │
  └───────────────┘        └───────────────┘        └───────────────┘        └───────────────┘
```

1. **Browse the Catalog**: Visit [shop24mart.com](https://www.shop24mart.com) on your browser or mobile phone.
2. **Choose Fulfillment**: Select your preferred local branch for pickup or enter your delivery address.
3. **Checkout with Ease**: Review your shopping bag and complete payment securely.
4. **Track & Enjoy**: Receive real-time dispatch updates until your order is safely delivered.

- **No App Store Download Required**: Tap **"Add to Home Screen"** on Chrome, Safari, or your preferred mobile browser.
- **Lightning Fast**: Blazing fast navigation, instant search, and minimal mobile data consumption.
- **Always Updated**: Always gives you the latest catalog and deals automatically.

<summary><strong>⚙️ Platform Engineering & Architecture (For Developers & Technical Teams)</strong></summary>

<br />

Shop24mart is powered by a high-throughput, enterprise omnichannel engine:

- **Frontend**: React 19, React Router v7, Vite, Tailwind CSS v4, TanStack Query v5, Zustand.
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express, TypeScript, Prisma ORM, MongoDB.
- **Realtime**: Socket.IO live order dispatch, Redis caching, BullMQ background queues.
- **Architecture**: Monorepo managed with Turborepo & pnpm workspaces.
